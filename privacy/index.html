Shorts Hub Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Shorts Hub is a personal tool that its owner runs on their own computer to upload and schedule the owner's own short videos to their own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects a TikTok account, the app receives the permissions the owner approves on TikTok (basic profile information, and the ability to upload, publish and list the owner's videos). It uses them only to post the owner's videos and show the owner's own video statistics.

Where data is stored

Login tokens, settings and post history are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the app, and nothing is shared with, sold to or used by third parties. The app does not collect data about any other person.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect an account at any time in the app, and can revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. Deleting the app's local data folder removes everything it has stored.
